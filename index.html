Questions for Lumen Health and working assumptions

What was assumed in place of an answer

There was no client to ask during the case study. Each row is a question that would have been put to Lumen Health, the answer the design assumes and what changes if that answer is wrong.

#QuestionWorking assumptionIf the assumption is wrong
1Is Lumen Health itself bound by HIPAA, or only the lab and EHR partner?Lumen Health handles all of the data as protected health information.The boundary between marketing data and clinical data can relax or has to tighten.
2Is the CRM replacement chosen? When does it arrive?Not chosen. It arrives during or after the 90 days.If the new platform has its own identity matching, the choice between buying and building reopens.
3Which system is the authority for each field: name, birthdate, email and consent?The EHR for name and birthdate, the portal for the login email and a separate store for consent.Merge rules and conflict handling change.
4Can family members legitimately share an email, phone or account? Can a parent register a kit for a child?Sharing is normal, so a shared email or phone never merges two records without other evidence.If accounts are per household, the model needs a household entity above the person.
5On a gift, who is the customer and who may see the results?The buyer owns the purchase. The recipient owns the kit and the results. The buyer never sees results.Kit roles and portal access change.
6What false-merge rate is acceptable? Who signs off on it?The privacy officer signs off. The rate is near zero for any link that touches a chart.The threshold moves and the size of the review queue moves with it.
7Who works the review queue? How many items a day can they take?Support does, at a volume in line with 26 items per 2,000 records.With no staff, uncertain pairs stay separate permanently and every consumer has to tolerate that.
8What are the daily order and registration volumes? How fresh must the marketing stage be?Thousands a day. A delay of minutes is acceptable.A real-time need or a much larger volume changes the matching approach.
9Can each system store an external identity key?Not reliably, so the identity service keeps the crosswalk and storing the key in a system is a late, optional step.If every system can store it, lookups get simpler and the service does less.
10Is consent recorded per person, per phone number or per channel?Per person per channel. The most restrictive value wins on a merge.Opt-outs tied to a number need different merge and split rules.

Run the identity slice

Python 3 standard library only

From the repository root, generate the two CSV outputs with:

cd claude
python3 -m identity --data ../data --out .

The current snapshot has 1,994 source records, 610 person keys and 26 review items covering 132 supporting record pairs. The 0.90 matching threshold is provisional, and scores are not calibrated probabilities. See the working slice README for the test command and limitations.

The records are fictional, but the brief requires treating the supplied data as protected health information. Treat derived identity outputs and review files as sensitive as well. Keep data/ and legacy-sync/ unchanged; they are the case-study evidence.
