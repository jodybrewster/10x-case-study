Lumen Health · Solution Architect Case StudyExecutive summary

The business problem

One person, many records, avoidable customer failures

Purchases, kit registrations and lab results move through separate commerce, CRM, portal and clinical systems. The current sync can attach an order to the wrong household account, overwrite contact details, block registrations and lose text opt-outs during account merges.

Create a small identity registry that links source records with evidence and confidence while each system remains responsible for its own data.

What we recommend

Give each person a stable internal ID. Keep source IDs, link evidence and correction history so uncertain matches can be reviewed and reversed.

Accept a kit registration immediately. Resolve identity in the background; keep clinical results and identity-dependent messages on hold until the patient link is reliable.

Separate the roles

A purchase can involve different people. The order, kit, registration and result should retain their own relationships instead of being forced onto one CRM account.

Buyer→Recipient→Registrant→Patient

These roles can be the same person or several people, especially for gifts.

What changes for the business

Fewer service failuresFewer orders sent to the wrong account and fewer registrations rejected by household-level duplicate rules.
Safer customer communicationMarketing sees an accurate lifecycle state and channel consent, not clinical result details. Opt-outs survive identity changes.
Steadier modernizationSystems can move to the new identity links in stages while orders and registrations continue to flow.

Introduce it without interrupting service

  1. 1 · ShadowSuggest links without changing production records.
  2. 2 · ReviewCheck uncertain and conflicting cases with operations.
  3. 3 · PilotUse confirmed links for selected workflows, with rollback.
  4. 4 · ExpandMove consumers over as accuracy and operations support it.

Track: incorrect merges, unresolved-match age, registration completion and delay, marketing-stage errors and opt-out preservation.

The case data is fictional and should be treated as protected health information for the design. Real deployment requires review of privacy, consent, vendor terms and the production integration paths. See the current-system diagnosis for code evidence, data checks and known unknowns.
