LUMEN HEALTHSolution architect case study

Executive summary

One customer, many systems, avoidable failures

Our four customer systems each keep their own idea of who a person is. The software that joins them guesses. Those guesses reach customers as rejected kit registrations, wrong marketing messages and texts to people who opted out.

Give every person one identity that sits outside the CRM. The software links records only when the evidence is strong, a person decides the uncertain cases and a kit registration is never held up by either.

What is going wrong

When an order's email is not recognised, the sync files it on any account with the same surname and zip code, often a relative's. Each order then overwrites that account's email. When the real owner registers a kit, the system cannot find them and the CRM rejects them as a duplicate of themselves.

Marketing reads its status from the same records, so it sends the wrong message. Merging duplicates loses text opt-outs. The records name genetic tests, so a purchase filed on a relative is a health-data exposure.

What changes for the business

CustomersA valid kit registration is always accepted at once. Gifts work. Messages match where the customer is.
SupportA short review queue replaces detective work: 26 open questions in the sample. Every merge can be undone.
MarketingOne status per kit, so nobody with results is asked to register. An opt-out survives every merge.
ComplianceTwo people are kept apart until the evidence or a reviewer says otherwise. Every link is recorded.

Introduce it in 90 days without interrupting service

  1. 1 · Days 1 to 14Fix the current software: the portal accepts every valid registration whatever the CRM says, the surname-and-zip guess goes, emails stop being rewritten.
  2. 2 · Days 15 to 45Run identity in shadow, changing nothing. Staff the review queue. Measure how often an automatic link is wrong before trusting any.
  3. 3 · Days 46 to 75Start using it, one system at a time with rollback: marketing, then the CRM, then the portal.
  4. 4 · Days 76 to 90Build the replacement CRM on the person key and retire the old sync.

Decisions needed: approve the two-week fixes and the plan, name who owns and staffs the review queue, have the privacy officer set the error rate at which automatic linking may be switched on and confirm that the strictest opt-out wins when records are combined.
